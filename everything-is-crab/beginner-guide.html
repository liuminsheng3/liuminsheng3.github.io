What is Everything is Crab? Brief intro

Everything is Crab is an imaginative and quirky indie game that transforms the world into a whimsical, crab-filled universe. Players take on the role of a crab navigating a vibrant, open world filled with various ecosystems, unique challenges, and eccentric characters. The game combines exploration, resource gathering, and light combat mechanics, all while emphasizing humor and creativity. Players can customize their crab, encounter other marine life, and engage in a variety of quests that explore themes of survival and friendship. With its captivating art style and engaging gameplay, Everything is Crab offers a unique experience that appeals to both casual gamers and hardcore enthusiasts alike.

In Everything is Crab, you can expect to:

  • Explore diverse environments like sandy beaches, rocky shores, and underwater caves.
  • Engage with quirky NPCs such as the wise old crab or the mischievous hermit crab.
  • Take on quests that range from gathering resources to battling rival sea creatures.

How to download and install

Getting started with Everything is Crab is straightforward, but you'll want to ensure you follow the correct steps for your platform. The game is available on multiple platforms, including PC, Nintendo Switch, and PlayStation.

For PC (Steam):

1. Open the Steam client on your computer.

2. Use the search bar to find Everything is Crab.

3. Click on the game title and select "Add to Cart."

4. Proceed to checkout and complete your purchase.

5. Once purchased, the game will appear in your library. Click "Install" to download.

For Nintendo Switch:

1. Open the Nintendo eShop from your home screen.

2. Search for Everything is Crab in the eShop.

3. Select the game and follow the prompts to purchase and download.

For PlayStation:

1. Navigate to the PlayStation Store from your console.

2. Search for Everything is Crab.

3. Select the game, purchase it, and download it to your system.

Regardless of your platform, ensure you have enough storage space and a stable internet connection for a smooth installation process. Once installed, you're ready to embark on your crabby adventure!

Game controls and basic mechanics

Understanding the controls and mechanics in Everything is Crab is essential for a successful start. The game features a simple control scheme, allowing players to immerse themselves in the vibrant world without getting bogged down by complex commands.

Basic Controls:

  • **Movement**: Use the WASD keys (or joystick on consoles) to navigate your crab.
  • **Attack**: Press the left mouse button (or R2 on consoles) to perform a claw attack.
  • **Interact**: Use the E key (or X button on consoles) to interact with objects and NPCs.
  • **Inventory**: Access your inventory using the I key (or the menu button on consoles) to manage items, weapons, and resources.

Core Mechanics:

  • **Resource Gathering**: Collect shells, seaweed, and other materials scattered throughout the world. These resources are vital for crafting and upgrading your gear.
  • **Combat**: Engage in battles with rival sea creatures, including the aggressive **Piranha Crab** and the elusive **Jelly Crab**. Each enemy has unique attack patterns, so learn their movements to dodge effectively.
  • **Quests**: Complete missions given by NPCs to earn rewards and progress through the game. Quests often involve gathering items or defeating specific enemies.

Familiarizing yourself with these controls and mechanics will set you up for success in Everything is Crab.

First 30 minutes - what to do

The first 30 minutes in Everything is Crab are crucial for setting the tone of your adventure. Here’s a step-by-step guide on what to focus on during this initial phase:

1. Create Your Crab: Customize your crab's appearance, choosing its color, claws, and accessories. This personalization enhances your connection to the character.

2. Learn the Basics: Follow the tutorial prompts to understand movement, combat, and gathering mechanics. Practice attacking small enemies like the Clam to get a feel for combat.

3. Explore the Starting Area: Familiarize yourself with the Sandy Shores, the first environment. Collect resources like Driftwood and Seaweed that are essential for crafting.

4. Complete Initial Quests: Speak to the Old Crab at the dock who will guide you through your first quest, typically involving collecting shells or defeating a small number of enemies. Completing these quests will reward you with experience points and items.

5. Upgrade Your Gear: Use the resources you've gathered to craft basic weapons, such as the Shell Club. This will prepare you for tougher enemies as you progress.

By focusing on these steps, you'll have a solid foundation to build upon as you delve deeper into the world of Everything is Crab.

Best beginner tips and tricks

As you embark on your journey in Everything is Crab, employing effective strategies can enhance your gameplay experience. Here are some of the best beginner tips and tricks to help you thrive in this crabby universe:

  • **Always Gather Resources**: Make it a habit to collect every resource you see. Items like **Coral Fragments** and **Crab Shells** can be used for crafting and upgrading equipment, which is essential for survival.
  • **Use Your Environment**: Take advantage of the different terrains. For instance, hiding behind rocks can help you avoid enemy attacks during combat, giving you a tactical edge.
  • **Learn Enemy Patterns**: Every enemy in **Everything is Crab** has a unique attack pattern. Take the time to observe how they move and attack; this knowledge will help you dodge effectively and counterattack.
  • **Prioritize Quests**: Focus on completing quests that yield high rewards. Some quests offer special items or significant experience boosts, which can fast-track your progression.
  • **Experiment with Crab Builds**: Don’t hesitate to try different crab builds. Mixing and matching gear can lead to unique playstyles that suit your preferences.

By implementing these tips, you’ll not only survive but thrive in the enchanting world of Everything is Crab.

Common mistakes to avoid

While diving into Everything is Crab, it’s easy to fall into pitfalls that can hinder your progress. Here are some common mistakes to watch out for:

  • **Ignoring Resource Management**: Many beginners neglect to manage their inventory effectively. Always keep an eye on your resources and make sure to craft necessary items before heading into tougher areas.
  • **Skipping Tutorials**: The tutorial may seem tedious, but skipping it can lead to confusion later in the game. Take the time to learn the mechanics thoroughly to avoid frustrating experiences.
  • **Overextending in Combat**: New players often rush into battles without understanding their enemy’s capabilities. Always assess the situation before engaging and retreat if necessary.
  • **Neglecting Upgrades**: Failing to upgrade your gear regularly can make combat significantly harder. Always check your inventory for resources and upgrade your weapons and armor.
  • **Forgetting to Save**: **Everything is Crab** allows for manual saving at various points. Make sure to save often, particularly after completing significant quests, to avoid losing progress.

By steering clear of these mistakes, you’ll enhance your overall experience and enjoy the delightful chaos of Everything is Crab.

How to progress fast

To progress quickly in Everything is Crab, you'll need to adopt effective strategies that will enhance your gameplay experience. Here are some actionable tips to accelerate your advancement:

1. Focus on Main Quests: Prioritize main story quests over side quests initially. Completing these will unlock new areas and provide valuable rewards that will aid in your progression.

2. Join a Crab Clan: As you progress, seek out clans and groups within the game. Joining a clan can provide access to exclusive quests and resources, accelerating your growth.

3. Utilize Fast Travel: Once unlocked, make sure to use the fast travel points scattered throughout the world. This will save you time and allow you to focus on completing quests efficiently.

4. Engage in Daily Challenges: Participate in daily challenges for bonus rewards that can help you level up your crab faster. These challenges often involve defeating certain enemies or gathering specific resources.

5. Collaborate with Other Players: If you’re playing in multiplayer mode, teaming up with friends can make challenging quests easier and more efficient. Coordination can lead to faster completion of objectives.

By employing these strategies, you’ll find yourself making remarkable progress in the whimsical world of Everything is Crab.

Quick Summary

  • **Familiarize yourself with the controls** and basic mechanics for a smoother experience.
  • **Gather resources** consistently to craft and upgrade essential gear.
  • **Prioritize completing main quests** to unlock new areas and rewards.
  • **Watch out for common mistakes** like neglecting inventory management and skipping tutorials.
  • **Utilize fast travel** and collaborate with other players to accelerate your progression.

Following these guidelines will ensure you have a rewarding and enjoyable journey in Everything is Crab!
