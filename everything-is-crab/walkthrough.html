Chapter/Level Overview

In Everything is Crab, players embark on a surreal adventure through various levels, each designed with unique challenges and environments. The game is divided into ten chapters, with each chapter introducing new mechanics, enemies, and lore.

1. Chapter 1: The Shimmering Shore

  • Introduction to basic controls and mechanics.
  • Key enemies: **Biting Barnacles** and **Sandy Scuttlers**.
  • Collectibles: **Shimmering Shells** scattered throughout the beach.

2. Chapter 2: Coral Catacombs

  • A darker, more maze-like environment.
  • Key enemies: **Shadow Crabs** and **Cursed Coral**.
  • Puzzle mechanics introduced: light reflection using **Crystal Claws**.

3. Chapter 3: The Abyssal Depths

  • Underwater challenges with air supply management.
  • Key enemies: **Giant Squid** and **Electric Eels**.
  • Collectibles include **Ancient Relics** that provide lore about the crab kingdom.

Each chapter of Everything is Crab has its unique flavor and obstacles. Be prepared to adapt your strategies as you progress, utilizing your skills and the environment to overcome challenges.

Boss Fight Strategies with Details

Boss fights in Everything is Crab are pivotal moments that require strategic thinking and precise execution. Here’s how to tackle the major bosses:

1. The King Crab (Chapter 5)

  • **Weakness**: Vulnerable to explosive attacks.
  • **Strategy**:
  • Use **Crab Cannon** to deal damage during his attack patterns.
  • Watch for his two primary attacks: the **Claw Slam** and **Bubble Barrage**. Dodge the Claw Slam by rolling away, and use the terrain to avoid bubbles.
  • After each attack phase, capitalize on the downtime with a series of rapid strikes.

2. The Coral Leviathan (Chapter 7)

  • **Weakness**: Exposed underbelly.
  • **Strategy**:
  • Utilize the **Spear of the Deep** for long-range damage when it breaches the water.
  • During its charge attack, use the **Dodge Roll** to evade and position yourself for a counterattack.
  • Collect and throw **Coral Shards** at its weak points for massive damage.

3. The Sandstorm Monarch (Chapter 9)

  • **Weakness**: Striking when stationary.
  • **Strategy**:
  • Avoid the whirlwind attacks by staying mobile and using cover.
  • When it stops to charge energy, unleash your strongest combo attacks.
  • Equip **Windbreaker Armor** for better resistance against its attacks.

Mastering these strategies will make your boss encounters in Everything is Crab more manageable and rewarding.

Puzzle Solutions

Everything is Crab features a variety of puzzles that challenge players to think creatively. Here’s how to solve some of the most intricate puzzles throughout the game:

1. The Light Reflection Puzzle (Chapter 2)

  • Objective: Direct light beams to unlock the gate.
  • Solution:
  • Position the **Crystal Claws** at angles to bounce the light.
  • Use the environment—look for reflective surfaces to maximize light exposure.
  • Ensure all beams converge on the gate simultaneously for it to open.

2. The Coral Maze (Chapter 4)

  • Objective: Navigate through a series of coral barriers.
  • Solution:
  • Use the **Map of the Lost** to highlight the correct path.
  • Activate **Crab Sense** to reveal hidden passages.
  • Avoid touching the coral walls, as they will reset your progress.

3. The Water Flow Mechanism (Chapter 6)

  • Objective: Redirect water to activate the ancient machinery.
  • Solution:
  • Adjust the valves in the correct order: **Left Valve (3 times), Right Valve (2 times)**.
  • Use the **Water Flow Boots** to traverse the currents safely.
  • Collect and place **Water Stones** at designated points to maintain flow.

These puzzle solutions will help you progress smoothly in Everything is Crab without unnecessary frustration.

Collectible Locations

Collectibles are a significant aspect of Everything is Crab, enhancing gameplay and lore. Here’s where to find key collectibles in each chapter:

  • **Shimmering Shells** (Chapter 1)
  • Locations:
  • Behind the first rock formation on the right.
  • Hidden under the large palm tree.
  • **Ancient Relics** (Chapter 3)
  • Locations:
  • Inside a sunken shipwreck; interact with the **Coral Key** to unlock it.
  • Near the Giant Squid boss arena, just before the entrance.
  • **Crab Coins** (Throughout the game)
  • Locations:
  • Defeating mini-bosses or completing side quests often rewards Crab Coins.
  • Check every nook and cranny; many are hidden in plain sight.

Collecting these items not only enhances your character’s abilities but also provides insights into the rich lore of the Everything is Crab universe.

Speed-run Tips

For those looking to break records in Everything is Crab, here are some speed-run tips that will optimize your playthrough:

1. Master Movement Mechanics:

  • Practice **Dodge Rolling** to navigate levels quickly without taking damage.
  • Use **Wall Jumps** in tight spaces to save time.

2. Skip Cutscenes:

  • Always skip cutscenes to save precious seconds. Familiarize yourself with the story so you can focus on gameplay.

3. Efficient Route Planning:

  • Pre-plan your route through each chapter to minimize backtracking.
  • Use the **Map of the Lost** strategically to find shortcuts and collectibles without losing time.

4. Boss Strategies:

  • Familiarize yourself with boss patterns to take them down quickly.
  • Use high-damage, single-target weapons to minimize fight duration.

5. Practice:

  • Record your runs to identify areas for improvement.
  • Join community forums to share tips and tricks with other speed-runners of **Everything is Crab**.

Implementing these speed-run strategies will enhance your efficiency and help you achieve faster completion times in Everything is Crab.
