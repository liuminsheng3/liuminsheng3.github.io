S-Tier: The Absolute Best, Explain Why

In Besmirch, S-Tier characters are the pinnacle of power and versatility. These heroes dominate in almost every game mode, boasting exceptional stats and abilities that can turn the tide in battle. Players looking to maximize their success should prioritize these characters.

Top S-Tier Characters

  • **Elysia**: With her **Arcane Burst** ability, Elysia can deal massive AoE damage while also providing team-wide shields. Her **Stat Boost** increases attack speed by 25%, making her a must-have in any team composition.
  • **Thorne**: Thorne excels in both offense and defense. His **Shield Wall** skill not only absorbs damage but also reflects a percentage back to attackers, making him a formidable foe.
  • **Kaelith**: Kaelith's **Elemental Fury** ability allows him to exploit elemental weaknesses, dealing double damage against specific enemy types. His high base stats make him a versatile choice in various scenarios.

Actionable Tips for Using S-Tier Characters

1. Synergize with Support Characters: Pair Elysia with healers like Maris, who can keep her in the fight longer.

2. Focus on Upgrading Gear: Equip S-Tier characters with high-level weapons such as the Dreadblade for increased damage output.

3. Adapt Your Strategy: Use Thorne’s defensive capabilities to draw enemy fire while your damage dealers take down key threats.

By focusing on these S-Tier heroes in Besmirch, you can ensure a higher win rate and a more enjoyable gaming experience.

A-Tier: Very Strong, Good in Most Situations

A-Tier characters are incredibly strong and can hold their own in most situations. While not as universally powerful as S-Tier heroes, they offer excellent utility and can be game-changers when used correctly.

Key A-Tier Characters

  • **Liora**: Her **Healing Wave** ability not only heals but also grants attack buffs, making her a dual-threat healer.
  • **Garruk**: With his **Berserker Rage**, Garruk can increase his damage output significantly, making him a great choice for aggressive strategies.
  • **Selene**: Selene’s ability to control the battlefield with her **Starlight Veil** can lock down enemies, providing your team with crucial advantages.

Tips for Maximizing A-Tier Characters

  • **Build Around Their Strengths**: Use Liora in compositions that require sustain, pairing her with damage dealers.
  • **Positioning is Key**: For Garruk, always ensure he’s in the fray to maximize his Berserker Rage damage.
  • **Crowd Control with Selene**: Utilize her abilities to zone enemies during critical moments, allowing for strategic retreats or pushes.

In Besmirch, A-Tier characters provide solid performance and should not be overlooked when forming your team.

B-Tier: Solid but Situational

B-Tier characters in Besmirch are solid picks but shine in specific scenarios. They can be useful when the right conditions are met, but generally, they fall short compared to higher-tier characters.

Notable B-Tier Characters

  • **Orin**: While strong, Orin's abilities are heavily reliant on specific enemy types, making him a situational pick.
  • **Fiora**: She offers decent damage but lacks the survivability needed for prolonged battles.
  • **Bran**: His **Fireball** skill has great potential but requires precise timing and positioning to maximize damage.

Strategies for B-Tier Characters

1. Utilize Their Niche Abilities: Use Orin when facing elemental foes to exploit his strengths against them.

2. Team Composition Matters: Pair Fiora with tanks who can soak damage, allowing her to deal damage from a safe distance.

3. Timing is Everything: For Bran, practice his Fireball timing to ensure it hits the maximum number of enemies.

While B-Tier characters can be effective in the right context, players should be cautious and strategic when including them in their lineup in Besmirch.

C-Tier: Niche Picks, Only in Specific Teams

C-Tier characters in Besmirch are niche picks that can be effective in very specific team compositions or against particular enemies. They often lack overall versatility but can shine in the right hands.

Example C-Tier Characters

  • **Dara**: While capable of decent damage, she requires specific team synergies to reach her full potential.
  • **Talon**: Talon’s abilities can be effective against certain foes, but his overall utility is limited.
  • **Iris**: Iris has a unique skill set but often finds herself outclassed by A and B-Tier characters.

Tips for Using C-Tier Characters

  • **Team Synergy is Crucial**: Build a team around Dara’s strengths to maximize her damage output.
  • **Focus on Matchups**: Use Talon against enemies that he counters effectively to gain an advantage.
  • **Practice with Iris**: Familiarize yourself with her abilities to exploit her strengths in niche scenarios.

Players should approach C-Tier characters in Besmirch with caution and be prepared to adjust their strategy based on team composition and enemy types.

D-Tier: Avoid Unless You Have No Better Option

D-Tier characters in Besmirch are generally considered weak and should be avoided unless you have no other options available. They often lack impactful abilities or suffer from poor stat distributions.

Characters to Avoid

  • **Nora**: While possessing unique abilities, her damage output pales compared to other characters.
  • **Zeke**: Zeke’s skills are underwhelming, making him a last-resort option.
  • **Riven**: Riven's lack of versatility and low base stats make her a poor choice for most teams.

Recommendations for D-Tier Characters

1. Use Sparingly: Only include D-Tier characters if you have no better options available.

2. Focus on Support Roles: If you must use a D-Tier character, consider putting them in a support role where their weaknesses are less impactful.

3. Stay Mobile: With weak stats, it’s crucial to keep D-Tier characters out of harm’s way to preserve their utility.

In Besmirch, players should aim to avoid D-Tier characters whenever possible to ensure a more favorable chance of success.

How Tier Lists Change with Updates

The landscape of Besmirch is ever-evolving, and tier lists can change significantly with updates. Developers often introduce balance changes that can buff or nerf specific characters, shifting their positions on the tier list.

Factors Influencing Tier Changes

  • **Balance Patches**: Regular updates can adjust character stats, change ability mechanics, or introduce new items that affect overall performance.
  • **Meta Shifts**: As players discover new strategies or team compositions, certain characters may become more valuable, leading to tier re-evaluations.
  • **Community Feedback**: Player feedback can prompt developers to adjust character abilities, ensuring a more balanced gameplay experience.

Staying Updated

1. Follow Community Discussions: Engage with community forums and social media platforms to stay informed about character viability.

2. Adapt to Changes: Be prepared to adjust your team composition as the meta evolves with each update.

3. Experiment: Don't hesitate to test new characters after updates to see if they fit into your strategies.

Keeping abreast of changes in Besmirch can enhance your gameplay experience and ensure that you are always fielding the best team possible.

Quick Summary

  • **S-Tier Characters**: Dominant heroes like Elysia and Thorne offer unmatched power and versatility.
  • **A-Tier Characters**: Strong options such as Liora and Garruk provide excellent utility and teamwork benefits.
  • **B-Tier Characters**: While solid, characters like Orin and Fiora are best utilized in specific scenarios.
  • **C-Tier Characters**: Niche picks that can excel in tailored team compositions but should be approached with caution.
  • **D-Tier Characters**: Generally weak options that should only be used if there are no better alternatives available.

By understanding the strengths and weaknesses of each character tier in Besmirch, players can make informed decisions that lead to greater success in their gaming endeavors.
