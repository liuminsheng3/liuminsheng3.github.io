Chapter/Level Overview

Each chapter in Besmirch presents unique challenges and environments that shape the gameplay experience. Understanding the layout and objectives of each level is crucial for success.

Chapter 1: The Forsaken Village

In this initial chapter, players are introduced to the mechanics of the game. The village is filled with lurking dangers, including Corrupted Beasts and environmental hazards.

  • **Main Objectives**:
  • Locate the **Ancient Tome** in the village square.
  • Rescue **Evelyn**, a crucial NPC who provides information on the **Dark Order**.

Chapter 2: The Ruins of Eldoria

Players venture into the ancient ruins, where puzzles and enemies become more complex.

  • **Main Objectives**:
  • Solve the **Rune Puzzle** to unlock the path to the **Guardian’s Chamber**.
  • Defeat the **Stone Sentinel** boss to progress.

Chapter 3: The Abyssal Depths

In this darker chapter, players must navigate treacherous waters and face the Drowned Wraiths.

  • **Main Objectives**:
  • Collect **Abyssal Pearls** to unlock new upgrades.
  • Survive encounters with the **Leviathan** to escape the depths.

Each chapter in Besmirch has its own lore and challenges, so familiarize yourself with the settings and objectives to enhance your gaming experience.

Boss Fight Strategies

Boss fights in Besmirch can be particularly challenging. Each boss has specific attack patterns that you need to learn to succeed. Here’s a breakdown of strategies for the major bosses.

The Stone Sentinel

  • **Location**: Ruins of Eldoria
  • **Strategy**:

1. Dodge its Stomp Attack: Watch for the wind-up and roll to the side.

2. Aim for Weak Points: The Sentinel has glowing spots on its arms and head. Use ranged attacks with the Longbow or Fire Staff to hit these weak points.

3. Summon Allies: Use the Summoning Sigil to call forth Spectral Warriors for additional damage.

The Leviathan

  • **Location**: Abyssal Depths
  • **Strategy**:

1. Avoid Water Spouts: When the Leviathan dives, it creates dangerous spouts. Stay clear of these to avoid heavy damage.

2. Use Heavy Artillery: Equip the Harpoon Gun for significant damage when it surfaces.

3. Timing is Key: Wait for the Leviathan to surface completely before attacking, as this is when it is most vulnerable.

By mastering these strategies, players can conquer the toughest foes in Besmirch.

Puzzle Solutions

Puzzles in Besmirch often require keen observation and critical thinking. Here are detailed solutions to some key puzzles.

Rune Puzzle in Eldoria

  • **Location**: Guardian’s Chamber Entrance
  • **Solution Steps**:

1. Identify the Symbols: Note the glowing runes on the pedestal.

2. Order of Activation:

  • Activate the **Phoenix Rune** first.
  • Follow with the **Dragon Rune**.
  • Finally, activate the **Serpent Rune**.

3. Confirm with the Light: Once activated correctly, a beam of light will illuminate the path forward.

The Mirror Maze

  • **Location**: Forsaken Village
  • **Solution Steps**:

1. Observe the Reflections: Identify which mirrors lead to the exit.

2. Shift Mirrors: Move the mirrors to redirect the light towards the central crystal.

3. Solve the Light Puzzle: Once the light hits the crystal, the exit will open.

These puzzles not only challenge your intellect but also reward you with valuable items and progression in Besmirch.

Collectible Locations

Collectibles are scattered throughout Besmirch, enhancing your gameplay with upgrades and lore. Here’s where to find some key items.

Ancient Tomes

  • **Location**: Forsaken Village
  • Found under the old oak tree near the village square.

Abyssal Pearls

  • **Location**: Along the shores of Abyssal Depths
  • Look for shimmering spots in the water; they are often guarded by **Drowned Wraiths**.

Hidden Artifacts

  • **Location**: Ruins of Eldoria
  • Behind the Stone Sentinel’s chamber, check the left wall for a hidden alcove.

By locating these collectibles, players can unlock powerful upgrades and lore that deepen the Besmirch experience.

Best Loadout for Each Section

Choosing the right loadout is crucial for success in Besmirch. Here’s a breakdown of the best configurations for each chapter.

Chapter 1: The Forsaken Village

  • **Weapons**:
  • **Short Sword** (quick attacks)
  • **Fire Staff** (area damage)
  • **Armor**:
  • **Leather Armor** (good mobility)
  • **Items**:
  • **Healing Potions** (essential for early encounters)

Chapter 2: The Ruins of Eldoria

  • **Weapons**:
  • **Longbow** (for range against the Sentinel)
  • **Fire Bombs** (for AoE damage)
  • **Armor**:
  • **Chainmail** (offers better protection)
  • **Items**:
  • **Summoning Sigils** (to call allies during boss fights)

Chapter 3: The Abyssal Depths

  • **Weapons**:
  • **Harpoon Gun** (high damage)
  • **Water Staff** (for utility)
  • **Armor**:
  • **Heavy Plate Armor** (for maximum defense)
  • **Items**:
  • **Potions of Agility** (to evade Leviathan's attacks)

Equipping the right loadout for each chapter allows players to optimize their effectiveness in Besmirch.

Speed-run Tips

For those looking to complete Besmirch quickly, here are essential speed-run tips to optimize your playthrough.

General Tips

  • **Practice**: Familiarize yourself with enemy patterns and puzzle solutions to minimize downtime.
  • **Skip Cutscenes**: Press the skip button to save valuable seconds.
  • **Route Planning**: Plan your route ahead of time, prioritizing objectives that unlock faster paths.

Specific Strategies

1. Chapter 1: Use the Short Sword for quick encounters and sprint past weaker enemies.

2. Chapter 2: Utilize the Longbow to take out enemies from a distance and avoid unnecessary fights.

3. Chapter 3: Collect Abyssal Pearls while dodging enemies instead of engaging—they’re not worth the time.

Mastering these speed-run techniques will drastically reduce your completion time in Besmirch.

Quick Summary

  • **Understand each chapter**: Familiarity with level layouts and objectives is key to success.
  • **Master boss strategies**: Learn attack patterns and optimal loadouts for each boss fight.
  • **Solve puzzles efficiently**: Knowing the solutions can save time and resources.
  • **Collectibles enhance gameplay**: Finding items boosts your character's abilities and lore.
  • **Optimize loadouts for chapters**: Equip the best weapons and armor for each section to maximize effectiveness.

With this comprehensive guide, players can navigate the intricate world of Besmirch with confidence and skill. Happy gaming!
