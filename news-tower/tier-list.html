S-Tier: The Absolute Best, Explain Why

In News Tower, S-Tier characters are the crème de la crème, offering unmatched versatility and power in virtually any situation. These characters excel in both offense and defense, making them essential for any player's roster. They often come equipped with unique abilities that can turn the tide of battle, ensuring they remain relevant throughout the game.

Top Picks:

  • **Evelyn the Editor**: With her ability to boost team morale, Evelyn not only increases the damage output of allies but also reduces cooldown times for special abilities. Her stats feature a staggering 120 attack power and 95 defense, making her a formidable opponent.
  • **Jay the Journalist**: Jay’s investigative skills allow him to reveal enemy weaknesses, granting allies a significant damage boost. His ability to gather intel makes him a strategic asset in team battles.

Actionable Advice:

  • Prioritize leveling up **Evelyn** and **Jay** as they can easily carry you through challenging missions.
  • Equip them with items like the **Press Pass** to enhance their abilities further, maximizing their effectiveness in **News Tower**.

A-Tier: Very Strong, Good in Most Situations

A-Tier characters in News Tower are robust and reliable options that can shine in most gameplay scenarios. While they may not have the overwhelming power of S-Tier characters, they bring a balanced skill set that can adapt to various challenges.

Notable Mentions:

  • **Charlie the Columnist**: His area-of-effect damage ability can clear out groups of enemies, making him an excellent choice for crowd control. With an attack stat of 105 and decent defense, he’s a great addition to any team.
  • **Maya the Multimedia Maven**: Known for her versatile abilities, Maya can switch roles smoothly between offense and support. Her healing skills can sustain the team during longer battles.

Actionable Advice:

  • Consider pairing **Charlie** with **Maya** to leverage their strengths for a balanced team composition.
  • Equip **Charlie** with **The Editor’s Quill** to boost his area damage, making him even more effective against hordes in **News Tower**.

B-Tier: Solid but Situational

Characters classified as B-Tier in News Tower are solid picks but may require specific team compositions to shine. They generally have good abilities but lack the versatility of higher-tier characters.

Key Characters:

  • **Nina the Newcomer**: While she has a high attack stat of 110, her abilities are more effective against certain enemy types. She shines against armored foes but struggles against agile opponents.
  • **Frank the Fixer**: His repair abilities are invaluable in prolonged battles, but his low mobility can be a liability in fast-paced scenarios.

Actionable Advice:

  • Use **Nina** in missions that feature more armored enemies to maximize her effectiveness.
  • Keep **Frank** on standby for longer missions where sustaining equipment is crucial.

C-Tier: Niche Picks, Only in Specific Teams

C-Tier characters in News Tower can be effective but are often limited to specific strategies or team setups. They may not perform well in a general context but can shine under the right conditions.

Examples:

  • **Lana the Layout Artist**: While she excels in creating advantageous battlefield layouts, her combat abilities are subpar. Use her in strategies focused on placement and control.
  • **Ricky the Rookie**: He has potential but lacks the stats to compete effectively in most battles. Pair him with high-damage dealers to compensate for his weaknesses.

Actionable Advice:

  • Consider forming a team around **Lana** that emphasizes control and area tactics to utilize her strengths.
  • Avoid using **Ricky** in solo missions; instead, team him up with A or S-Tier characters to elevate his utility.

D-Tier: Avoid Unless You Have No Better Option

D-Tier characters in News Tower are often underwhelming and can hinder your progress if included in your lineup. They typically have low stats and abilities that offer minimal impact on gameplay.

Characters to Avoid:

  • **Sammy the Scribbler**: With a mere 70 attack and low mobility, Sammy struggles to keep up in fights. His abilities do not compensate for his lack of power.
  • **Olivia the Observer**: While she has some utility in scouting, her combat abilities are lackluster, making her a poor choice for most encounters.

Actionable Advice:

  • Limit the use of **Sammy** and **Olivia** to early game scenarios where you’re forced to fill gaps in your roster.
  • Focus on upgrading other characters rather than investing time or resources into D-Tier options.

How Tier Lists Change with Updates

The landscape of News Tower is ever-evolving, as game developers frequently release updates that can significantly alter character balance. New abilities, changes in stats, and the introduction of new characters can shift the tier list over time.

Key Factors Influencing Changes:

  • **Balance Patches**: Regular updates often adjust character stats, which can elevate a previously low-tier character to a more competitive level.
  • **Meta Shifts**: As new strategies and team compositions emerge, certain characters may become more or less effective, necessitating a reevaluation of their tier status.

Actionable Advice:

  • Stay updated with patch notes to understand how your favorite characters may have been affected.
  • Be flexible in your team composition; regularly reassess your roster based on the current meta trends in **News Tower**.

Quick Summary

  • **S-Tier** characters like **Evelyn** and **Jay** are essential for any team, providing unmatched versatility and power.
  • **A-Tier** characters such as **Charlie** and **Maya** are reliable and can adapt to various situations effectively.
  • **B-Tier** characters are solid but best utilized in specific scenarios, while **C-Tier** and **D-Tier** characters should generally be avoided unless absolutely necessary.
  • Changes from updates can shift the tier list, so keep an eye on character balance and meta trends to stay competitive in **News Tower**.
