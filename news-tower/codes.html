Code Redemption Limits and Restrictions

When it comes to redeeming codes in News Tower, there are certain limits and restrictions that players should be aware of. Understanding these can help you maximize your benefits and avoid any potential issues.

Key Limitations:

  • **One-Time Use**: Most codes in **News Tower** are designed for one-time use per account. Once you’ve redeemed a code, it cannot be used again, even if you try it on a different account.
  • **Account Restrictions**: Some codes may be tied to specific accounts or regions. If you encounter a code that seems valid but doesn’t work, it may be restricted to certain players.
  • **Expiration Dates**: Codes often come with expiration dates. If a code has expired, you won’t be able to redeem it, so make sure to use them promptly.

By being aware of these limitations, you can ensure a smoother experience when redeeming codes in News Tower.
