What is Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse? Brief intro

Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse is an immersive action-adventure RPG that invites players into a hauntingly beautiful world filled with magic, mystery, and complex lore. Set in the cursed land of Eldoria, players take on the role of the mysterious witch, Elara, as she embarks on a quest to lift the dark curse plaguing her homeland. With a mix of engaging storytelling, challenging combat, and intricate puzzle-solving, Never Grave offers a unique gaming experience that keeps players coming back for more.

As you explore the diverse environments, from the enchanted Whispering Woods to the treacherous Ruins of Lost Hope, you’ll encounter various NPCs, each with their own backstories and quests. Combat mechanics are fluid, with a rich system of spells and abilities that allow for customizable playstyles. Whether you prefer to wield powerful spells or engage in melee combat, Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse provides the tools needed to create your ideal witch. This guide aims to help you navigate your early hours in the game, ensuring you start your journey on the right foot.

How to download and install

Getting started with Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse is straightforward, whether you’re playing on PC, PlayStation, or Xbox. Here’s how to download and install the game efficiently:

1. Visit Your Platform's Store:

  • For PC: Open Steam or the Epic Games Store.
  • For PlayStation: Access the PlayStation Store.
  • For Xbox: Go to the Microsoft Store.

2. Search for the Game:

  • Use the search bar to type in “**Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse**.”

3. Purchase and Download:

  • Click on the game and follow the prompts to purchase it. After purchase, select the ‘Download’ option.
  • Ensure you have enough storage space (the game requires approximately 30GB).

4. Install the Game:

  • Once the download is complete, the installation will begin automatically.
  • After installation, launch the game from your library or home screen.

5. Updates:

  • After installation, check for updates to ensure you have the latest version, which may include bug fixes and additional content.

With the game installed, you're ready to dive into the enchanting world of Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse.

Game controls and basic mechanics

Understanding the controls and mechanics of Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse is crucial for mastering its gameplay. Here’s a breakdown of the controls for PC and console:

PC Controls:

  • **WASD**: Move character
  • **Left Mouse Button**: Basic attack
  • **Right Mouse Button**: Use equipped spell
  • **Spacebar**: Dodge roll
  • **E**: Interact with objects/characters
  • **Q**: Open inventory
  • **Tab**: Open map

Console Controls:

  • **Left Stick**: Move character
  • **Square/X**: Basic attack
  • **Circle/B**: Use equipped spell
  • **X/A**: Dodge roll
  • **Triangle/Y**: Interact with objects/characters
  • **Options/Menu**: Open inventory
  • **Touchpad**: Open map

Basic Mechanics:

  • **Energy System**: Your spells consume energy. Monitor your energy bar to avoid being caught without spells during combat.
  • **Health and Healing**: Collect herbs to craft potions. Always keep a few potions in your inventory for tough encounters.
  • **Leveling Up**: Gain experience points (XP) through quests and combat. Leveling up increases your stats and unlocks new abilities.

By familiarizing yourself with these controls and mechanics, you’ll be well-equipped to handle the challenges that Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse throws your way.

First 30 minutes - what to do

Your initial moments in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse are critical for establishing a solid foundation. Here’s a step-by-step guide on what to prioritize in your first 30 minutes:

1. Complete the Tutorial: Follow the guided tutorial to learn the basics of movement, combat, and spellcasting. Pay close attention to the hints provided.

2. Explore the Starting Area: Familiarize yourself with your surroundings. The Enchanted Glade is your first area, filled with resources and NPCs. Interact with them to pick up side quests.

3. Gather Resources: Collect herbs and materials scattered throughout the area. These will be essential for crafting potions and upgrading your equipment later on.

4. Level Up: Focus on completing early quests to gain XP. Aim to reach level 2 or 3 before venturing too far. This will unlock new spells and abilities crucial for combat.

5. Practice Combat: Engage in battles with the local wildlife, like the Shadow Wolves, to hone your combat skills. Experiment with different spells and find a playstyle that suits you.

6. Map Familiarization: Open your map (press Tab on PC) to get a sense of the layout. Mark locations of interest, especially quest givers and resource hotspots.

By strategically utilizing your first half hour in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse, you’ll set yourself up for successful exploration and combat in the game.

Best beginner tips and tricks

Starting strong in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse can significantly enhance your gaming experience. Here are some invaluable tips and tricks for beginners:

  • **Always Save Your Game**: Use save points frequently. The game allows you to save at specific locations - don’t hesitate to do so after completing significant tasks or before entering dangerous areas.
  • **Experiment with Spells**: There are various spells to unlock, including **Frostbolt** and **Fireball**. Try different combinations to find what works best against enemies and for solving puzzles.
  • **Upgrade Your Gear**: Early on, gather materials to craft better gear. Focus on upgrading your staff, as it enhances spell power. Look out for components in the **Whispering Woods**.
  • **Join a Faction**: As you progress, consider joining one of the factions like the **Circle of Elders**. This can provide access to unique spells and equipment.
  • **Use the Environment**: Many battles can be influenced by your surroundings. Use obstacles to block enemy attacks or lure them into traps.
  • **Complete Side Quests**: Don’t ignore side quests. They provide valuable rewards, XP, and insights into the game’s lore, which can enrich your experience.

By following these tips, you’ll enhance your gameplay in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse and prepare yourself for the challenges that lie ahead.

Common mistakes to avoid

New players often fall into traps that can hinder their progress in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse. Here are common mistakes to avoid:

  • **Neglecting Inventory Management**: Players often hoard items without crafting or using them. Regularly check your inventory and craft potions and upgrades to avoid being overwhelmed.
  • **Ignoring NPCs**: Skipping conversations with NPCs can lead to missed quests and lore. Engage with characters to uncover hidden stories and side quests that can benefit your journey.
  • **Overlooking the Map**: Failing to utilize the map can lead to unnecessary backtracking and confusion. Regularly check the map to navigate effectively and mark points of interest.
  • **Underestimating Enemies**: Some players rush into fights without understanding enemy mechanics. Take time to learn enemy attack patterns, especially with tougher foes like **Cursed Knights**.
  • **Spending All XP Immediately**: While it’s tempting to spend all your XP on immediate upgrades, save some for later levels to unlock more powerful abilities.

By avoiding these common pitfalls, you can ensure a smoother and more enjoyable experience in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse.

How to progress fast

Progressing quickly in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse requires strategic planning and efficient gameplay. Here are some techniques to help you level up and advance your story rapidly:

1. Focus on Main Quests: Prioritize main story quests over side quests initially. Completing these will provide significant XP boosts and unlock new areas.

2. Utilize Fast Travel: Once you unlock fast travel points, use them to quickly navigate the map. This saves time and allows you to complete quests efficiently.

3. Engage in Combat Wisely: Choose your battles carefully. Focus on enemies that provide the best XP rewards, like Cursed Beasts and mini-bosses.

4. Join a Guild Early: Joining a guild not only provides access to exclusive quests but also grants unique gear and spells that can significantly enhance your combat capabilities.

5. Daily Objectives: Take advantage of daily objectives that reset each day. These often provide substantial rewards that can accelerate your progress.

6. Crafting and Upgrading: Regularly craft and upgrade your gear. Higher-level gear can make combat easier, allowing you to take down tougher enemies more effectively.

By implementing these strategies, you can achieve rapid progression in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse, making your gameplay experience more rewarding.

Quick Summary

  • **Engage with NPCs and complete side quests** for lore and rewards.
  • **Experiment with spells and combat styles** to find your preferred playstyle.
  • **Avoid common mistakes** like neglecting inventory management and underestimating enemies.
  • **Focus on main quests** and utilize fast travel to optimize progression.
  • **Craft and upgrade gear regularly** for better combat effectiveness.

With this beginner guide, you are now prepared to embark on your journey through Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse. Enjoy the adventure!
