Chapter/Level Overview

In Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse, players embark on a riveting journey through a dark fantasy world filled with haunting landscapes and intricate lore. The game is structured into several chapters, each introducing unique environments, enemies, and narrative elements.

Chapter Breakdown

1. The Forsaken Woods: Start your adventure in a gloomy forest filled with twisted trees and lurking foes. Here, you will learn the basics of combat and gather your first weapons.

2. The Abandoned Village: As you progress, you will arrive at a desolate village where you must interact with NPCs and uncover clues to advance the story.

3. The Ruined Castle: Challenge yourself with tougher enemies and elaborate traps as you explore the castle's dark corridors, searching for the Cursed Relic.

4. The Witch’s Lair: The final chapter where you confront Morwenna, the titular witch, and face your greatest challenges.

Each chapter is designed to gradually increase in difficulty while immersing players deeper into the narrative of Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse. Ensure you explore every nook and cranny to uncover secrets that enhance your capabilities for the battles ahead.

Boss Fight Strategies

Facing bosses in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse is a thrilling yet challenging experience. Each boss has unique abilities and weaknesses that can be exploited for victory.

Key Bosses

  • **The Corrupted Knight**:
  • **Weakness**: Fire-based attacks.
  • **Strategy**: Use **Fireball** spells and dodge his melee attacks. Stay mobile to avoid getting caught in his area-of-effect slam.
  • **Morwenna, the Witch**:
  • **Weakness**: Holy artifacts.
  • **Strategy**:

1. Equip the Holy Sword for increased damage.

2. Use ranged attacks to avoid her Shadow Spells.

3. Watch for her spellcasting animations to counterattack effectively.

Tips for Success

  • Always have a few **Healing Potions** on hand.
  • Upgrade your weapons before each boss encounter, focusing on those that exploit the boss's weaknesses.
  • Learn the boss's attack patterns through trial and error; patience is key in these fights.

Puzzle Solutions

Puzzles in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse are cleverly integrated into the environmental design, often requiring players to think critically and explore their surroundings.

Notable Puzzles

1. The Enchanted Door:

  • **Location**: Abandoned Village.
  • **Solution**: Find the **Ancient Key** hidden in a nearby well. Interact with the door after obtaining the key to gain access to hidden treasures.

2. The Elemental Runes:

  • **Location**: Ruined Castle.
  • **Solution**: Align the runes based on their elemental properties (Fire, Water, Air, Earth) to unlock the next area. Use the hint from the wall mural for guidance.

Tips for Solving Puzzles

  • Thoroughly explore each area for clues and items.
  • Take notes on any hints you find; they may not seem relevant at first but can link to multiple puzzles.
  • Collaborate with friends online to discuss potential solutions, as community input can often spark new ideas.

Collectible Locations

Collectibles are crucial in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse as they enhance your gameplay experience and provide valuable upgrades.

Types of Collectibles

  • **Lore Books**: Provide backstory and insights into the world.
  • **Hidden Artifacts**: Upgrade your abilities.
  • **Currency**: Used for crafting and purchasing items.

Key Locations

  • **The Forsaken Woods**:
  • Collect the **Lost Journal** near the old tree stump.
  • **The Abandoned Village**:
  • Search the mayor's house for **The Town's History** and various **Gold Coins**.
  • **The Ruined Castle**:
  • Find the **Ancient Artifact** in the hidden chamber behind the throne room.

Collectible Hunting Tips

  • Use a guide to track down elusive items.
  • Keep an eye out for shimmering objects; they often indicate hidden collectibles.
  • Explore areas thoroughly before progressing to the next chapter, as some collectibles may be inaccessible later.

Best Loadout for Each Section

Choosing the right loadout in Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse can significantly impact your gameplay experience. Here’s a breakdown of the best equipment for each chapter.

Recommended Loadouts

| Chapter | Weapons | Armor | Items |

|----------------------|-----------------------|---------------------|---------------------------|

| The Forsaken Woods | Wooden Sword, Bow | Light Armor | Healing Potions, Firebomb |

| The Abandoned Village | Steel Sword, Crossbow | Medium Armor | Healing Potions, Smoke Bombs|

| The Ruined Castle | Holy Sword, Magic Staff| Heavy Armor | Healing Potions, Mana Elixirs|

| The Witch’s Lair | Legendary Sword | Enchanted Armor | Ultimate Healing Potion, Buff Elixirs |

Loadout Tips

  • Always upgrade your weapons between chapters to maintain combat effectiveness.
  • Choose armor based on your playstyle; if you prefer agility, opt for lighter armor.
  • Stockpile items as you progress, ensuring you're prepared for tougher enemies and bosses.

Speed-Run Tips

For those aiming to complete Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse in record time, mastering the mechanics and knowing the game inside out is crucial.

Speed-Run Strategies

1. Master Movement: Utilize the Dash ability to navigate through areas quickly while avoiding enemy encounters.

2. Skip Cutscenes: Familiarize yourself with the story to skip unnecessary cutscenes and dialogues.

3. Optimal Routes: Learn the fastest paths through each chapter, avoiding backtracking when possible.

4. Use Fast Travel: Unlock fast travel points early, allowing for quicker navigation across the map.

Speed-Run Resources

  • Watch speedrun videos to learn from the best players.
  • Join forums and communities dedicated to speedrunning for tips and strategies.
  • Practice specific sections to improve your timing and efficiency.

Quick Summary

  • **Explore Thoroughly**: Each chapter of **Never Grave: The Witch and the Curse** is filled with lore, collectibles, and hidden secrets.
  • **Boss Strategies**: Understand each boss's weaknesses and prepare your loadout accordingly for maximum effectiveness.
  • **Puzzle Solutions**: Pay attention to environmental clues, as they often hint at the solutions to puzzles.
  • **Optimal Loadouts**: Choose weapons and armor that suit your playstyle to excel in combat and exploration.
  • **Speed-Run Techniques**: Master movement and learn the fastest routes to complete the game efficiently.
