How to Get Free Rewards in Monster Hunter Wilds - All Working Methods

Daily login rewards and how to maximize them

One of the easiest ways to accumulate free rewards in Monster Hunter Wilds is through the daily login rewards system. Every day you log in, you receive various bonuses that can include currency, crafting materials, and even rare items. Here’s how to make the most out of this feature:

  • **Consistency is Key**: Aim to log in every day. Streak bonuses increase the rewards over time, so don’t miss a day. For example, logging in for seven consecutive days can yield up to **500 gems** and exclusive crafting materials.
  • **Check Your Calendar**: The game often has special events tied to daily logins. These can provide additional rewards, like limited-time gear or exclusive items. Keep an eye on the **in-game calendar** for these opportunities.
  • **Timing Matters**: Log in during peak hours to potentially catch surprise bonuses or double rewards events. The developers sometimes announce these through social media or in-game announcements.

By consistently utilizing the daily login rewards in Monster Hunter Wilds, players can build a formidable inventory without spending a dime.

Free currency farming methods

In Monster Hunter Wilds, currency is essential for crafting gear and upgrading your hunter. Fortunately, there are several methods to farm free currency effectively:

  • **Complete Quests**: Regularly engage in quests, especially those marked as daily or weekly. Completing these can reward you with **gold** and **gems**, essential currencies for upgrading your equipment.
  • **Participate in Expeditions**: Taking part in expeditions not only helps you gather resources but also rewards you with currency based on the monsters you hunt. Focus on high-tier monsters for better payouts.
  • **Monster Hunts**: Target specific monsters known for dropping lucrative materials. For instance, hunting the **Elder Dragon** can yield rare loot that sells for a high price.
  • **Treasure Hunts**: Keep an eye out for treasure chests during your adventures. These chests can contain a variety of currencies, crafting materials, and sometimes even rare items.

By employing these strategies in Monster Hunter Wilds, you’ll be able to build up your currency reserves without spending real money.

Event rewards calendar

Staying up-to-date with the event rewards calendar in Monster Hunter Wilds can significantly boost your gameplay experience. Events often come with exclusive challenges that offer unique rewards. Here’s how to leverage them:

  • **Check Regularly**: Events change frequently, so make it a habit to check the in-game calendar or the official website for announcements. Events often include limited-time monsters, crafting materials, and exclusive gear.
  • **Participate in Seasonal Events**: These events, like the **Winter Festival** or **Summer Bash**, usually provide unique cosmetics, currency bonuses, and even powerful weapons. For example, completing the **Winter Festival challenges** can net you exclusive winter gear.
  • **Collaborate with Friends**: Some events encourage collaboration with other players, offering bonuses for team achievements. Gathering a group can help you complete these challenges faster and maximize your rewards.
  • **Track Your Progress**: Use the in-game event tracker to monitor your progress. Completing all tasks often leads to bonus rewards, making it worth your time.

By actively participating in the event rewards calendar in Monster Hunter Wilds, players can unlock powerful gear and currency, enhancing their hunting capabilities.

Achievement rewards worth grinding for

Achievements in Monster Hunter Wilds not only add to your bragging rights but also yield valuable rewards. Here’s a breakdown of some noteworthy achievement rewards worth your time and effort:

  • **Master Hunter**: Completing a certain number of hunts grants you **rare crafting materials**. Aim for this achievement early on to bolster your crafting capabilities.
  • **Conqueror of the Wilds**: This achievement rewards players with a unique title and a **special weapon** once you defeat ten high-ranking monsters. It's a great way to show off your skills.
  • **Collector**: Gather a full set of a specific armor type to receive exclusive crafting items. For example, collecting the entire **Dragonhide Armor Set** can reward you with powerful stats and an aesthetic bonus.
  • **Challenge Accepted**: Completing specific challenges, like defeating a monster using only certain weapons, can yield extra gems and crafting materials.

Grinding for these achievement rewards in Monster Hunter Wilds can significantly enhance your gameplay, providing you with the resources to upgrade your hunter effectively.

Referral and friend bonus systems

Monster Hunter Wilds offers a robust referral and friend bonus system that can help you earn additional rewards by inviting others to play. Here’s how to make the most of these systems:

  • **Invite Friends**: When you refer a friend to join **Monster Hunter Wilds**, both you and your friend can receive bonuses, including gems and exclusive items once they reach certain milestones.
  • **Daily Friend Bonuses**: Playing with friends can yield daily bonuses. For example, you might receive additional currency or crafting materials for hunting with a buddy.
  • **Team Challenges**: Engage in team-based challenges that reward both you and your friends for collaborative gameplay. Completing these challenges can lead to significant rewards, including rare items.
  • **Social Media Promotions**: Keep an eye on the game’s social media channels for special referral bonuses. Sometimes, they run promotional events that give even more significant rewards for inviting new players.

By leveraging the referral and friend bonus systems in Monster Hunter Wilds, you can not only enhance your own gameplay but also help your friends discover the game while reaping rewards together.
