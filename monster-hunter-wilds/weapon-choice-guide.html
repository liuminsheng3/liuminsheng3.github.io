Quick Answer

Step-by-Step Test Route

Use this route when you want a clean answer instead of another vague recommendation. It is intentionally small so you can repeat it after a patch or after unlocking a new Monster Hunter Wilds system.

  1. Run a training hunt
  2. Track how often attacks whiff
  3. Check whether you can punish wounds safely
  4. Commit to ten hunts before switching again

How to Judge the Result

A change is worth keeping only if it improves a real outcome: fewer failed attempts, cleaner resource use, faster recovery from mistakes, better team coordination, or a more reliable clear. If the result only looks stronger on paper but makes your run less stable, keep the old plan.

  • Keep the change if it solves the repeated failure.
  • Rollback if the run feels harder to execute.
  • Retest after a major patch, event, or balance update.
  • Add hands-on notes when you can verify exact numbers or routes.
