How to redeem codes (step by step)

Redeeming codes in Phantom Blade Zero is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to ensure you claim your rewards without any hassle:

1. Launch the Game: Start Phantom Blade Zero on your device.

2. Access the Main Menu: Once in the game, navigate to the main menu.

3. Locate the 'Settings' Option: Look for the settings icon, usually represented by a gear symbol.

4. Find the 'Redeem Code' Section: Within the settings, there should be a specific section for code redemption.

5. Enter the Code: Type in your desired code carefully. Ensure there are no typos or extra spaces.

6. Confirm the Redemption: After entering the code, click the confirm button. You should see a message indicating whether the code was successfully redeemed or not.

7. Check Your Rewards: Exit the menu and check your inventory to verify that the rewards have been added.

By following these simple steps, you can quickly take advantage of the benefits that codes offer in Phantom Blade Zero.
