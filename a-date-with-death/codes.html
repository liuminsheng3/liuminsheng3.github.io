1. Launch the Game: Open A Date with Death on your device.

2. Access the Main Menu: Once you’re in the main game interface, navigate to the settings or options menu. This can usually be found in the upper right corner of the screen.

Code Redemption Limits and Restrictions

Understanding the limitations and restrictions surrounding code redemption in A Date with Death is vital for maximizing your rewards. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

  • **One-Time Use**: Most codes can only be redeemed once per account. If you've already used a code, it cannot be used again.
  • **Account Restrictions**: Some codes may be region-locked or limited to specific game accounts (like new player bonuses). Always check the code's description for any such limitations.
  • **Expiration Dates**: Codes often have a deadline for use. This expiration may not always be clearly stated, so it’s beneficial to redeem codes as soon as they are released.
  • **Character-Specific Codes**: Certain codes may only unlock rewards for specific characters. For example, a code might only provide a skin for **Lila** and not for **Jack** or other characters.

Being aware of these limits will help you strategize your code redemption efforts and maximize the benefits you gain from your time spent in A Date with Death.
