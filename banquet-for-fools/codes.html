1. Launch the game: Open "Banquet for Fools" on your device.

2. Navigate to the Settings Menu: Click on the gear icon located in the top right corner of the main screen.

3. Select the 'Codes' option: This will open the code redemption interface.

4. Enter the code: Type in one of the active codes exactly as shown (case-sensitive).

5. Confirm your entry: Click the 'Redeem' button to submit the code.

Code Redemption Limits and Restrictions

Understanding the limits and restrictions surrounding code redemption in "Banquet for Fools" is essential for optimizing your gameplay. Here are some key points to consider:

  • **One Redemption Per Account**: Most codes can only be used once per account. If you attempt to redeem a code again, you will receive an error message.
  • **Time-Sensitive Codes**: Certain codes are only available for a limited time, often tied to in-game events or promotions. Ensure to redeem them before they expire.
  • **Regional Restrictions**: Some codes may be restricted to specific regions. Always check if a code is applicable to your account based on your location.
  • **Character-Specific Codes**: A few codes may grant rewards exclusive to particular characters or classes, so be sure to check if the rewards align with your playstyle.
